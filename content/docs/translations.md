# Translate Horizon

Help translate Horizon’s in-game menus and settings. Fix one awkward line or help review a whole language.

## Get started

1. [Open Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/horizon-client) and choose your language.
2. Pick a string to translate, or check existing suggestions and vote for ones that read well.
3. If the context is unclear, leave a comment in Crowdin, ask in [#translations](https://discord.com/channels/1559227979002028052/1569353842317856778), or skip the string.

## Writing a translation

- **Use consistent terms.** Search Crowdin for how the project already translates the same word.
- **Write naturally.** Translate the meaning and adapt idioms. If the source is ambiguous, prefer singular, general wording and an informal tone where that fits your language.
- **Preserve names and formatting.** Keep product names such as Horizon and Launcher, brand names, code variables, placeholders such as `{0}` and `%s`, and line breaks intact.
- **Translate by hand.** AI may be used for dictionary lookups only. Submitting machine- or AI-generated translations can get you removed from the project.
- **Review in context.** Check where the string appears when you can, and only approve wording you’ve checked yourself. Keep feedback respectful and focused on the translation.

## Help review

Find your [language team](https://discord.com/channels/1559227979002028052/1574140487655231568) if you’d like to help review. Proofreader roles are based on consistent quality and at least 300 approved strings.

Found a mistake in Horizon? Share the language, where you saw it, and what you’d change in [#translations](https://discord.com/channels/1559227979002028052/1569353842317856778).
